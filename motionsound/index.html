How to play

  • Tilt left for lower notes, right for higher notes. Small tilts are enough.
  • Tilt the top of the phone away from you to get louder, toward you to get quieter.
  • Turn around (or spin the phone flat on a table) to make the tone brighter or darker.
  • Move quickly to add wobble and sparkle. Shake for a burst of notes. Shake harder for a bigger burst.
  • Reset / Calibrate makes your current position the middle.

Simulation mode

Drag in the square: left/right is pitch, up/down is volume. Or use the sliders.
