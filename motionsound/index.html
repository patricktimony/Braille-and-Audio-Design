How to play

  • Tilt left for lower notes, right for higher notes.
  • Tilt the top of the phone away from you to get louder, toward you to get quieter.
  • Turn the phone flat, like a steering wheel on a table, to make the tone brighter or darker.
  • Shake the phone to play a chime.
  • Reset / Calibrate makes your current position the middle.

Simulation mode

Drag in the square: left/right is X tilt, up/down is Y tilt. Or use the sliders.
