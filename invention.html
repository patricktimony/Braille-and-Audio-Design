Spoken Diff

Paste or open two versions of a text. Spoken Diff lists every change in plain words, such as: Line 4: “Tuesday” replaced with “Wednesday”, after “meeting on”. That way you can review edits with a screen reader, a braille display or the keyboard alone. It doesn't rely on colour. Nothing leaves your computer.

Shortcuts anywhere on the page: F8 or Alt+J for the next change, Shift+F8 or Alt+K for the previous change. Each change is also a level 3 heading, so you can use your screen reader's heading keys.

    How it works

    Spoken Diff first matches whole lines, then compares the changed lines word by word. Each change names its line number, what was removed or added, and the words just before it so you can find the spot. Punctuation and spacing changes are named, such as comma removed or 2 spaces became 1 space. Changes that only affect capital letters are reported as capitalization changes. If most of a line is different, the whole line is reported as rewritten, with the old and new versions.

    Your texts are kept in this browser so they're still there when you come back. They are never sent anywhere.
