# Scan Reader: how to use it

*(For a helper to read aloud, or to print in large type or Braille.)*

**Starting:** the reader starts by itself when the computer is switched on and
you are signed in. You will hear "Scanner reader is ready."
If it isn't running, press **Control, Alt and S** together.

**Reading a page**
1. Lift the scanner lid. Put the page **printed side down** on the glass,
   with the top of the page toward the hinge. Close the lid.
2. Press **F5**. (**Enter** works too.)
3. You will hear "Scanning", then "Recognizing text", then "Reading", then the page.
4. For the next page, put it on the glass and press **F5** again.

**While it reads**
- **Space**: pause. Press **Space** again to carry on from the same word.
- **Escape**: be quiet.
- **Up arrow / Down arrow**: hear the line before or after.
- **Left arrow / Right arrow**: hear the word before or after.
- **Page Up / Page Down**: go to the page before or after.
- **Minus / Plus**: slower or faster voice.
- **F1**: hear the list of keys and which page you are on.

**If something goes wrong,** the reader will tell you what to do. If it says
the page looks blank, turn the page over. If it cannot find the scanner,
check the cable.

**Starting a new letter or book:** press **Control and N**, then press it again.
Your earlier pages are kept.

Everything you scan is saved automatically. Your helper can find it in
**Documents → Scanned Pages**.
