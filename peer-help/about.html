About Peer Tech Help

Peer Tech Help connects blind and low-vision people who want to learn technology with people who can teach them. It is open source and free to run.

What is real and what is a demonstration

  • Real: your profile, your help requests, the trainer matching, your saved instructions, and the speech features. All of these work, and are stored only in this browser.
  • Demonstration: every trainer named "(demo)", their messages, and the example requests and library entries. No real person reads or answers your messages in this version.
  • Not built yet: sign-in, accounts, sending messages to real people, and AI. Instructions are made by simple rules on your device, not AI.
  • External: the voice call link opens Jitsi Meet, a separate free service with its own privacy policy.

Privacy

  • There is no sign-in. Everything you enter is kept in this browser's storage on this device, and is not sent to any server.
  • Nothing is recorded. Transcripts are only used when you tick the agreement box, and only on your device.
  • Instructions are private. They appear in the Shared Library only if you choose to share them and confirm they contain no personal information.
  • You can delete everything on the My Profile page.

Using a keyboard, screen reader, or your voice

  • Every page starts with a "Skip to main content" link, and uses headings, so you can move by heading (H with JAWS and NVDA, the rotor with VoiceOver).
  • Everything works with the Tab, Enter, Space, and arrow keys. There are no menus to open and nothing to drag.
  • Speech input is optional. Buttons that say "Speak" fill in a text box that you can also type in. If your browser cannot do speech input, use your device's dictation instead.
  • Voice Control on iPhone and Voice Access on Windows can press any button by saying its name, for example "Tap Get Help".

Opening from Morphic

Organisations that use the Morphic toolbar can add a button that opens this site. See the project README for the configuration file.
