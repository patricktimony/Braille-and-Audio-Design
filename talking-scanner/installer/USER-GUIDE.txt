Talking Scanner user guide

Talking Scanner reads printed pages aloud. Put a page face down on a flatbed
scanner, press one key, and the page is read to you. It works offline and
uses only the keyboard. You do not need a mouse, an account, or the internet.


1. Installing

Download the installer from this address:
https://github.com/patricktimony/Braille-and-Audio-Design/releases/latest/download/TalkingScannerSetup.exe

Your browser may ask whether to keep the file. In Microsoft Edge, press
Control plus J to open downloads. If Edge says the file is not commonly
downloaded, move to the file, open its More actions menu, and choose Keep,
then Keep anyway.

Open the downloaded file. Windows may show a message called
"Windows protected your PC". This appears because the installer is new and
not yet known to Microsoft. Press Tab until you hear "More info", and press
Enter. Then press Tab until you hear "Run anyway", and press Enter.

The installer speaks each step aloud. If JAWS, NVDA or Narrator is running,
the installer stays quiet and your screen reader reads it instead.

The installer has two steps:
  Step one: press Enter to install.
  Step two: press Enter to finish. Talking Scanner then starts by itself.

No administrator password is needed. Nothing else needs to be installed.


2. Starting Talking Scanner

Press Control plus Alt plus T at any time.
Or press the Windows key, type Talking Scanner, and press Enter.
There is also a Talking Scanner shortcut on the desktop.

The first time, Talking Scanner asks you to choose your keys.
If your keyboard has a number pad, press the Enter key at the bottom right
of the number pad. Otherwise, press the main Enter key.


3. Keys

Every action is one key. Both sets of keys always work.

  Action                    Number pad          Main keyboard
  Scan and read             Number pad Enter    Enter
  Play or pause             5                   Space
  Previous or next word     4 or 6              Left or Right arrow
  Previous or next line     8 or 2              Up or Down arrow
  Previous or next page     7 or 9              Page Up or Page Down
  Help                      F1                  F1
  Quit                      Escape              Escape

When you quit, Talking Scanner asks whether to keep the pages.
Press Y to save them in the folder "Talking Scanner Books" in your user
folder, or N to delete them.


4. Using JAWS

JAWS and Talking Scanner both speak. Talking Scanner reads the page with
its own Windows voice, and JAWS keeps working as usual.

JAWS uses the number pad for its own commands while Num Lock is off.
If you use the number pad keys, turn Num Lock on. If JAWS is running and
Num Lock is off, Talking Scanner reminds you of this.
The main keyboard keys (arrows, Space, Enter) work with JAWS either way.

To hear the last thing Talking Scanner said, press Insert plus T to read the
window title. The title always holds the last announcement.


5. Using Narrator

Narrator and Talking Scanner both speak. To hear the last announcement
again, press Narrator key plus T.

If the arrow keys or Space seem to do nothing, Narrator's scan mode is
probably on. Press Caps Lock plus Space to turn it off.

You may like to turn on this Narrator setting: "Lower the volume of other
apps when Narrator is speaking".


6. Practice and checks, without a scanner

In the Start menu, in the folder Talking Scanner Tools:

  Talking Scanner practice with sample pages
    Practise all the keys with four sample pages. No scanner is needed.
  Talking Scanner key test
    Speaks the name and action of every key you press.
    Press Escape twice to finish.
  Talking Scanner self test
    Checks that text recognition and speech work on this computer,
    then says "Self test passed" or "Self test failed".
  Uninstall Talking Scanner


7. Scanners

Talking Scanner uses the scanner's normal Windows driver. If Windows did
not install a driver when you plugged the scanner in, install the driver
from the scanner maker's website. You do not need the maker's scanning app.

If there is a problem, such as the scanner being unplugged, switched off
or busy, Talking Scanner tells you in plain words.


8. What has been tested

Tested automatically on Windows, before every release:
  installing and uninstalling, using only the keyboard;
  every key, with Num Lock on and off;
  text recognition on the sample pages, including an upside-down page;
  the Windows voices;
  that the window can be read by screen readers (UI Automation).

Not tested yet:
  any physical scanner;
  use with JAWS or Narrator by a person;
  speech through real speakers.
Please tell us how it works for you.


9. Privacy

Nothing is ever sent to the internet. Scanned pages are deleted when you
quit, unless you press Y to save them.


10. Removing Talking Scanner

Use "Uninstall Talking Scanner" in the Talking Scanner Tools folder of the
Start menu, or Windows Settings, Apps, Installed apps.
Your saved books are kept.
