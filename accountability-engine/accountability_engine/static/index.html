Overview

The Accountability Engine helps you investigate unexplained transactions, spot patterns of questionable billing, and gather evidence for a dispute. It separates what your records prove from what is only possible and what is still unknown.

  • Everything runs on this computer. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on external AI and give consent in Privacy and settings.
  • The app never contacts banks, merchants or regulators, and never starts a dispute. It only drafts letters for you to review.
  • It never calls a charge theft or fraud. A finding is a question worth checking.

    Help

    Moving around

    • Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between links, buttons and fields. Every control works with Enter or Space.
    • The main navigation is a list of links near the top. After you choose one, focus moves to that page's heading.
    • Screen reader users can jump by heading (H in NVDA and JAWS, or the VoiceOver rotor). Findings and investigation sections each start with a heading.
    • Status messages, such as “Import finished”, are announced automatically.
    • There are no single-key shortcuts, so nothing triggers by accident.

    What the words mean

    Verified fact
    Backed by an imported record or arithmetic on imported records.
    Interpretation
    A reading of text, such as what a prefix in a description usually means.
    Hypothesis
    A possible explanation. Not confirmed. Each one says how to check it.
    Unknown
    Something the records cannot answer, and where you might find the answer.
